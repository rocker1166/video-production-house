# Style: Beatcut (stock footage cut on a song's beats)

Stock-footage travel reels cut on a song's beats. The clip library and song store are yours to bring (they are large and licensed per user);
this card is the law that survives across brands; the workflow is skill `reel-factory`, the structures are `reels/FORMATS.md`.

- 8-19 s typical; cuts on the beat (0.45-0.75 s grid for fast songs, 1.5-2 s slow). Plain cuts, no fancy transitions.
- Movement lives inside the shot (drone push, drift, someone walking away). People are seen from behind.
- The song starts at the part people know. Align to the reference's HITS and quiet GAPS (`cues.json`), never to a bare beat grid.
- Text is small. Hinglish original copy; never copy a reference's words.
- 16 formats (`drone`, `flip`, `voice`, `solution`, `itinerary`, `recap`, `listicle`...) (drone hold, myth/contrast flip, voiceover list, pain->solution, itinerary, group recap, listicle...).
- Corrections so far (ledger r003): fonts, no silent copy, number the files, keep jpgs out of `out/`.
- Songs: do not bake a licensed song into a post; post with the platform's own "Use audio".
