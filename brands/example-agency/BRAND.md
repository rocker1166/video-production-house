# Northwind Studio (example brand, agency niche)

A worked example of a brand whose goal is **growing the agency**, not selling a consumer product. It is hypothetical; replace every line with your own.

## Product and promise
Websites and brand systems for local service businesses. Promise: a site that brings enquiries, shipped in four weeks.

## Audience and pain
Owners of plumbing, dental, legal and cleaning firms. Pains in their words: "my site is from 2015", "I get calls but not from the website", "my last agency vanished", "I do not know what I am paying for".

## Voice
Founder to camera or screen, plain, specific, numbers only when real and permitted.

## Claims and compliance
No guaranteed results, no client names or logos without written permission, no prices in public posts.

## Visual world
Screen recordings and before/after crops of real work; paper-editorial look (`vox`) for explainers.
