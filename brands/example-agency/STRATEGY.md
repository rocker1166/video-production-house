# Northwind Studio strategy (EXAMPLE: hypotheses to show the shape, not evidence)

## Goal -> outcome -> audience behaviour
Goal: "grow my agency". Business result: 3 qualified enquiries a month. A stranger must notice (a hook that names their pain), trust (proof it works for businesses like theirs), then ask (a DM).

## Offer and proof
Offer: website + brand system, 4 weeks. Proof we have: 3 finished sites. Proof missing: written permission and numbers from at least one client. **Producing that proof is the first task**, because the trust stage has nothing to stand on without it.

## Platform mechanics
Instagram names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as top Reels signals (secondary sources; newer signals unverified). So: hooks that earn a send ("send this to the owner who needs it") and openings that hold 3 seconds.

## Content types
See `workflow.json`: reach-hook, story-proof, authority-explainer, offer-cta, conversation. Starting mix 50/35/10/5, shifted toward proof because trust is this agency's bottleneck (an inference, to be tested).

## Cadence and KPIs
3 posts a week. Reach: sends per 1k views, 3 s hold. Trust: saves, profile visits. Conversion: DMs. Ignore: raw likes and follower count.

## First 3 experiments
1. Five reach-hooks on the same pain with different openings. Wrong if sends per 1k do not differ after 5 posts each.
2. One before/after story-proof. Wrong if saves and profile visits match a plain hook.
3. One teardown carousel. Wrong if saves per 1k are below the reels.

## Capability gaps
Trend research (outlier reels in the niche), post analytics into the ledger, draft-only scheduling, voiceover.
