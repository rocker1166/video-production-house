# <Brand> : brief for the factories

Fill this once with `brand-onboard`; both `reel-factory` and `carousel-factory` read it before they make anything.

## Product and promise
What it does, for whom, in one sentence. The one thing a viewer should remember.

## Audience and pain
Who watches. Their 5 sharpest, most relatable frustrations, in their own words (these become hook lines and "pain words").

## Voice
Language, register, humour level, what the brand would never say. Two example captions that sound right, two that sound wrong.

## Claims and compliance
What may NOT appear (prices, offers, medical/financial promises, competitor names, real people's names on screens). Required labels.

## Visual world
Palette, fonts, the look of the approved style, sample posts the owner loves. Real footage/screens available (and which are safe to publish).

## Proof and sources
Where footage, screenshots and facts come from. Anything unverified must be marked as such on delivery.

## Learned rules
Appended by the loop: `date · rule · why (their words)`. Newest at the bottom.
