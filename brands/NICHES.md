# Niche playbooks (how the same workflow adapts)

Only **travel** is proven (about 30 reels, 8 carousels, real verdicts). The rest are hypotheses built by mapping the same structures onto the niche: treat them as `untested` until a run is approved, then edit the row.

| Niche | Subject | Hook archetypes that should transfer | First 3 reel formats | First carousel | Claims trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| travel (proven) | a place | pain-words -> place at the drop; myth/contrast flip; one-shot drone hold | `drone`, `solution`, `flip` | how it works in 6 slides; one-region art style | no prices, no real names on screens |
| SaaS / app (untested) | a workflow or screen | pain words -> product at the drop; before/after; "stop doing X" | `solution`, `flip`, `story` | problem -> fix, real screens in the phone frame | no unverified numbers, no customer names |
| fitness (untested) | an exercise or routine | one-line mood over a hard rep; listicle of mistakes; pov | `mood`, `listicle`, `pov` | 5 mistakes / 5-step routine | no medical or weight-loss promises |
| food (untested) | a dish | single hero shot (the `drone` of food); steps; pov | `audio`, `itinerary`, `title` | recipe in 6 slides | no health claims, credit the source |
| ecommerce (untested) | a product | before/after; unboxing pov; "why this" listicle | `flip`, `pov`, `listicle` | gift guide, use-cases | no price or stock claims, honest reviews only |
| education (untested) | a concept | myth-buster; one-minute explainer; story with punchline | `flip`, `voice`, `story` | concept in 6 slides | cite sources, no guaranteed outcomes |

Add a niche with `brand-onboard`: fill the row, run the pilot pair, edit the row from the verdict.
